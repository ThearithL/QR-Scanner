QR colorsChoose a clean style

Recommended: 512 px for daily sharing, 1024 px for extra clarity, 2048 px for print. The selected size is saved on this device.

For reliable scanning, keep the code dark and the background light. Avoid gradients and low contrast.

Made for sharing

Create Wi‑Fi access cards, event links, contact numbers, and more. Never put sensitive information in a QR you plan to share publicly.

Wi‑Fi tip
A Wi‑Fi QR contains your network name and password. Share it only with people you trust.
Scan tip
A QR code can hide a misleading link. Check the full address before opening it.
